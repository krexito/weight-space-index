- **Low-bit trace residuals (`Qwen3.5-0.8B`, `scripts/experiment_trace_lowbit.py`, CPU, 2026-11-23, `benchmarks/trace_quant_sweep/results_lowbit_trellis_cpu.json`, negative):** goal was INT8 quality at ~2-3 bits. 3 saved GSM8K generations (750-814 tok, 2 with loops), all 24 block outputs, same 64-wide Hadamard rotation; codecs: uniform `int8`/`int4` (tracer's own), Lloyd-Max scalar `lm4/lm3/lm2`, and a QTIP-style bitshift trellis (`tcq3` L9k3 3.34 b/el, `tcq2` L8k2 2.34 b/el; LUT fitted by Viterbi+Lloyd on the non-loop sequence). Seq 101: `int8` 8.25 b relL2 `0.0056`, KL `3e-5`, top-1 `99.4%`, onset moved `0/24` layers; `int4` 4.25 b `0.102` / `1e-2` / `96.3%` / `10/24`; `lm4` `0.094` / `9.5e-3` / `7/24`; `tcq3` `0.142` / `1.9e-2` / `94.0%` / `16/24`; `lm3` `0.179` / `3.3e-2` / `17/24`; `tcq2` `0.272` / `7.3e-2`; `lm2` `0.328` / `0.12` (other two sequences the same within 0.001 relL2). The trellis does beat scalar at equal rate (about 2 dB: relL2 `0.142` vs `0.179` at 3 bits) but every 2-4 bit codec is 17-60x worse than INT8 and moves the loop onset on 7-20 of 24 layers. Why no codec can reach it: after the rotation the residual is close to i.i.d. Gaussian, where the rate-distortion bound gives relL2 >= 2^-R, so INT8's `0.0056` needs >= ~7.5 bits for any codec that treats elements independently (the bound at 3.34 b is relL2 `0.099`; `tcq3` gets `0.142`, ~3 dB away, so a better trellis could gain at most that). The structure a smarter codec could use is small: a layer needs `545-766` of `814` SVD components for INT8-level error, and the change from the previous layer is still `0.37-0.75` of the residual norm (worth about 1 bit). Verdict: keep `int8+had64`; the best realistic gain is ~25% (about 6 bits with cross-layer prediction), not 2-3x. Note: `onset` at thr 0.96 is fragile; even `int4` moves it, so a looser use (no exact onset) could live with 4 bits.
